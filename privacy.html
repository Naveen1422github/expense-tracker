kharchly · privacy

What kharchly stores

Your expenses, people and budgets. They are stored only on your device, in your browser's storage.

Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, kharchly saves backup files into a Kharchly folder in your own Google Drive. It uses Google's drive.file permission, so it can see only the files it created, and nothing else in your Drive.

What we don't do

  • There is no kharchly server and no account.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your data is never shared with anyone. The developer cannot see your data.

Disconnecting

In the app: Settings → google drive → disconnect. Or in your Google Account: Security → third-party connections → kharchly → remove access. Deleting the Kharchly folder in Drive removes the backups.
